## 11. How is Buddhism different from Hinduism?

Another major difference is their approach to conversion and identity. Buddhism spreads through proselytism and conversion, with followers taking Refuge in the Three Jewels: Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha[4]. Hinduism, by contrast, is an umbrella term for a range of Indian religious traditions that are unified by adherence to Dharma, and its origins are tied to the Vedas[2].

Finally, their scale and status differ. Buddhism has about 320 million followers and is the world's fourth-largest religion[1], while Hinduism has around 1.2 billion followers and is described as the oldest surviving religion in the world[2].
