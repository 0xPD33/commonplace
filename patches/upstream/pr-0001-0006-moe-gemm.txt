Title: ggml-cpu : repack MUL_MAT_ID with GEMM on groups of 4 routed rows, and dynamic chunks

forward_mul_mat_id calls one gemv per routed token per expert, even in a 512-token prompt, and splits every expert's
columns statically across threads. This patch packs each expert's routed rows 4 at a time (q8_0x4, the same
ggml_quantize_mat_t the dense path uses) and calls the repacked gemm; the rows left over keep the gemv. Columns of all
used experts are then handed out in chunks of whole NB_COLS groups through the threadpool chunk counter, like the dense
path, so fast cores take more work on mixed-core phones.

Each output is computed with the same kernels as before (gemm vs gemv only change how many rows share a tile), and each
column by one thread: on x86, 120 prompts through our app gave word-for-word identical answers, and KL divergence on
Ling-3.0-tiny is at the tool's floor; the same on arm64 under qemu-aarch64.
