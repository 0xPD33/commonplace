CIA World Factbook, final edition (pack factbook)

CREDIT
Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), The World Factbook: https://www.cia.gov/the-world-factbook/
Each article is one country or area. Its page is at https://www.cia.gov/the-world-factbook/countries/<country name in lower case, words joined by hyphens>/
The JSON mirror is the repository factbook/factbook.json (https://github.com/factbook/factbook.json), commit 144d697.

CHANGES
This pack is an adapted copy. The JSON data was converted to text lines of the form "Field: value", one section per Factbook category, and cut into passages. Photographs, maps and flags were not included.
