OpenStax textbooks (pack openstax)
Source: Hugging Face dataset HuggingFaceTB/openstax_paragraphs (snapshot 2024-01-29), a copy of the text of openstax.org books.

CREDIT
OpenStax, Rice University: https://openstax.org
Each article is one chapter section of an OpenStax book. The title reads "<book>: <chapter>". Each article stores the page of its book on openstax.org (https://openstax.org/details/books/<slug>). Where you read a book at https://openstax.org/books/<slug>, OpenStax asks users to credit it as: "Access for free at https://openstax.org/books/<slug>".
The authors of each book are named on its page at openstax.org and in the book front matter.

These books were published under CC BY 4.0 when the text was copied (2024-01-29), as the license file of each OpenStax book repository shows (https://github.com/openstax). In March and April 2026 OpenStax changed the license of the current editions of many books to CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. This pack holds the earlier text under the CC BY 4.0 license that applied to it; Creative Commons licenses are irrevocable for the copies that were already given out. Newer editions at openstax.org can have other terms.
OpenStax books that were already under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 at that date are not in this pack: Calculus Volumes 1, 2 and 3, Principles of Accounting Volumes 1 and 2, Business Law I Essentials and Organic Chemistry.
Third-party material in a book (for example some images) can have other licenses. This pack holds text only.
This pack is a collection. Each book keeps its license. The pack as a whole is not placed under a new license.
