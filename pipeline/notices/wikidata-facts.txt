Wikidata facts (pack wikidata-facts)
Source: the Parquet tables permutans/wikidata-* (a conversion of the Wikidata dump of 2026-05-07; snapshot 2026-05-07).

CREDIT
Wikidata contributors, https://www.wikidata.org
Each item has a QID Q. The item page is at https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q and its history is at https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Q&action=history

CHANGES
This pack is an adapted copy. It keeps items that have an English Wikipedia page, a selected set of properties, labels and aliases in English. Statements were flattened into a SQLite table.
